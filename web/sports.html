      A stream won’t play?

      Fantasy Stream doesn’t supply broadcasts; it plays links you can already watch. Direct video and .m3u8 links work best. If a site blocks playing here, use Open stream under the player or pick another source.

      ESPN connection

      Public leagues need only the ID. A private league also needs SWID and espn_s2 from your ESPN cookies (on espn.com: developer tools → Application → Cookies). They stay in this browser and go only to ESPN. Nothing here changes your ESPN lineup.

      How automatic switching works
      • Follows the teams of your active fantasy players, live games first.
      • Switches only at a reported break (timeout, intermission, end of an inning) or when a game ends.
      • Pick a game yourself and the channel stays there until the next break.
      • Exact substitutions and provider ads can’t be seen from here.
