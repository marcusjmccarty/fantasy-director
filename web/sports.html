    A stream won’t play?

    Fantasy Stream doesn’t supply broadcasts; it plays links you can already watch. Direct video and .m3u8 links work best. Some sites block playing inside another page: use Open stream under the player, or choose another source for that game. Website players may need one Play click after a game change.

    How automatic switching works
    • Follows the teams of your active fantasy players, live games first.
    • Switches only at a reported break (timeout, intermission, end of an inning) or when a game ends.
    • Pick a game yourself and the channel stays there until the next break.
    • Exact substitutions and provider ads can’t be seen from here.
