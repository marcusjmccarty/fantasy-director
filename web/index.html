        A stream won’t play?

        Fantasy Stream finds third-party video links; it doesn’t host video and isn’t affiliated with ESPN or any sports league. Availability, permission to watch, and playback depend on the provider. NTV’s player opens here and may open pop-up ads when clicked. If it won’t play, try Reload stream or another source. Automatic switching can change embedded players, but some providers require another click to start video.

        Third-party sites may show ads. Don’t install anything they ask for, and close the tab if it looks wrong. Connecting a private ESPN league needs a computer.

        Private league?

        On a computer, sign in to espn.com and open your browser’s developer tools (F12, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac). Choose Application (it may be hidden behind >>), then Cookies, then espn.com. Copy SWID and espn_s2 here, then press Connect ESPN. Phones can’t do this step: add your players by hand instead.

        They’re saved in this browser only. Each time your league or scores load, they pass through Fantasy Stream’s server to reach ESPN, and the server doesn’t keep them. Treat them like a password: they sign in to your ESPN account.

        How the channel decides
        • Only games where one of your starters is on the field, offense first and closest to the end zone. Your D/ST fills in when no offense is on.
        • Kickers only on 4th down in field-goal range (inside the opponent 42).
        • Never cuts mid-play or right before a snap. A rival drive needs to be 10+ yards closer, or entering the red zone, to take over.
        • A score stays on screen for the celebration. Timeouts and commercials are skipped when another game is available.
        • Pick a game yourself and the channel stays until the next break. When none of your starters are on, the channel follows the best scoring chance among games with streams.

        If a cut lands on the wrong moment, pause on a snap and add a mark with the quarter and clock shown on screen. Your marks take priority.
