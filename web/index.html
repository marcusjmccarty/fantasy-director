      How automatic switching works
      • Follows your starters’ teams using possession and field position. Individual player substitutions are not tracked.
      • Your game selection comes first. Automatic switching resumes at a detected break, the end of the game, or a stream failure.
      • In automatic mode: offense first, closest to the end zone. Your D/ST only fills in when no offense is on.
      • When none of your starters are active, live RedZone mode follows the closest scoring chance among your saved streams, even after your team’s games finish.
      • Kickers only on 4th down in field-goal range (inside the opponent 42).
      • Tries to wait for the end of a play before switching. A rival drive needs to be 10+ yards closer, or entering the red zone, to take over.
      • During a detected break, other available games can fill in. Use “Skip this break” for a commercial we cannot detect. Short score replays finish before automatic switching.

      Each replay is aligned to the play-by-play with about two hundred scorebug readings taken from its footage. If a cut still lands on the wrong moment, pause on a snap and add a mark: the quarter and game clock on screen, at the current video position. Your marks take priority. Alignment is estimated; a recording can still contain commercials.
