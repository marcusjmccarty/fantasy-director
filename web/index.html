        Settings

        Set it so the clock in the corner of the channel matches the scorebug on your stream. The channel then follows the game as you see it: switching and score alerts timed to your picture. Timing is approximate and can vary between streams.

        How the channel decides

        • Follows your starters’ teams using possession and field position. Individual player substitutions are not tracked.
        • Offense first, closest to the end zone. Your D/ST only fills in when no offense is on.
        • Kickers only on 4th down in field-goal range (inside the opponent 42).
        • Tries to wait for the end of a play before switching. A rival drive needs to be 10+ yards closer, or entering the red zone, to take over.
        • Detected breaks and supported provider ads are skipped. Missed scores are replayed when matching footage is available.

        Each replay is aligned to the play-by-play with about two hundred scorebug readings taken from its footage. If a cut still lands on the wrong moment, pause on a snap and add a mark: the quarter and game clock on screen, at the current video position. Your marks take priority.
