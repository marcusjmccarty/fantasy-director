      A stream won’t play?

      Fantasy Stream doesn’t supply broadcasts; it plays links you can already watch. Direct video and .m3u8 links work best. Some sites block playing inside another page: use Open stream under the player, or try another source for that game. Website players may need one Play click after a game change.

      Settings

      How the channel decides
      • Only games where one of your starters is on the field, offense first and closest to the end zone. Your D/ST fills in when no offense is on.
      • Kickers only on 4th down in field-goal range (inside the opponent 42).
      • Never cuts mid-play or right before a snap. A rival drive needs to be 10+ yards closer, or entering the red zone, to take over.
      • A score stays on screen for the celebration. Timeouts and commercials are skipped when another game is available.
      • Pick a game yourself and the channel stays until the next break. When none of your starters are on, RedZone follows the best scoring chance among games with streams.

      Each replay is aligned to the play-by-play with about two hundred scorebug readings. If a cut still lands on the wrong moment, pause on a snap and add a mark with the quarter and clock on screen. Your marks take priority.
