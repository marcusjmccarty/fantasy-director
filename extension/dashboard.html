    CONTROL ROOM

    League & settings

    Use Chrome 120 or newer. Sign in to ESPN in the same browser profile, then connect. No server or API key is required. The live engine runs in a dedicated background worker while you watch.

    Optional: import ESPN cookies

    Only needed if browser sign-in does not work. Import replaces SWID and espn_s2 in this Chrome profile’s ESPN cookie store. These fields are cleared after submission and never saved in the project.

    LIVE ENGINE & TIMING

    Keeps tied or incomplete feeds from staying on one game indefinitely. Red-zone drives and higher-priority roster opportunities remain protected. Rotation also respects the routine shot hold.

    Urgent roster and scoring opportunities bypass the routine hold after a two-second transition guard. Live polling targets five seconds by default; delays and rate limits can extend it. If cuts are already late, keep the offset at zero. Positive offsets only help when ESPN is ahead of your video. You can tune each assigned stream separately.

    Live roster action and red-zone threats take priority. Clips return to coverage when they finish. Chrome may start muted; Enable sound in the replay player once if needed.

    Published MP4 clips play in the lightweight replay player. Clips without a playable source retain their ESPN link. Automatic clips are at most 90 seconds, with a two-minute break between replays during live viewing. Highlights honor your stream delay buffer.

    Stop restores the mute settings captured before this session. Automatic switching focuses stream tabs and their windows. Use a dedicated viewing window if you’re working elsewhere. Emergency stop: Option/Alt + Shift + S.

    Forget clears the extension’s settings and session data. It does not sign you out of ESPN. Never share your ESPN cookies or a browser profile containing them.
